      About This Project

      Student Analytics is a lightweight, front-end-only college management dashboard. It lets students view their attendance and academic performance, and lets administrators manage students, record attendance, enter marks, and review analytics — all without a backend.

      Features

      • Student dashboard with attendance & marks
      • Tabbed admin panel (6 sections)
      • Attendance marking & marks entry
      • Charts, analytics & CSV export
      • Data persisted with localStorage
